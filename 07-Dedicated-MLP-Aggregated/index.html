07 — Dedicated Per-Drug Models on Aggregated Data

10 independent Per-drug models (LR, RF, MLP) on pooled A+B+C+D. This is the most comprehensive non-federated baseline, producing the hyperparameters reused by the Federated learning study (08).

Sections

  • Results and Outputs
  • Loss Curves for the 07 Hyperparameters
  • Integration with Federated Learning (08)

Objective

Run a full systematic comparison of all three model families (LogisticRegression, MLP, Random Forest) across all 10 drugs on pooled data, establishing:

  1. The best achievable Aggregated (pooled) training performance per drug

  2. Optimal hyperparameters per drug per model — saved to best_params.csv and reused by 08

  3. The performance ceiling that Federated learning can aim to recover

Notebooks

  • 07-Dedicated-MLP-Aggregated.ipynb — Main: LR + RF + MLP for 10 drugs

  • 07-01-Dedicated-LR-MLP-Aggregated.ipynb — LR + MLP only (no RF)

  • 07-02-Dedicated-MLP-ClassWeight.ipynb — MLP with class weight (ran 2026-10-15; see the class-weight ablation in Results and Outputs)

  • 07-02a-Prep-ClassWeight-Inputs.ipynb — rebuilds 07-02’s input caches without MLP training

  • 07-03-Dedicated-RF-Fast.ipynb — Faster RF variant (ran to produce the RF columns)

Models

Model

Hyperparameter Search

Output

LR

L2 regularization, GridSearchCV(C), Threshold Tuning

Per-drug C, threshold

RF

RandomizedSearchCV (n_iter=20, cv=2, 5000-row subsample) over n_estimators, max_depth, min_samples_leaf, class_weight, plus Threshold Tuning (see 07-03)

Per-drug params, threshold

MLP

8×8 GridSearchCV lr×dropout, internal val, Early stopping (patience=10)

Per-drug lr, dropout

Split

species-stratified split 70/15/15 on pooled A+B+C+D per drug.

Preprocessing

log1p transform + Standardize to zero mean (fit on train only).

Key Findings

  • Mean test performance across the ten drugs on Aggregated (pooled) training: LogisticRegression 0.788 BalAcc / 0.862 AUC, MLP 0.784 / 0.848, Random Forest 0.761 / 0.843.

  • LR and MLP are effectively tied, with the per-drug wins split: the MLP leads on Ciprofloxacin (+0.017), Amoxicillin-Clavulanic acid (+0.008), Piperacillin-Tazobactam (+0.009) and Amikacin (+0.010 BalAcc); LR leads on Gentamicin, Imipenem, Ceftazidime and Vancomycin.

  • RF is below the two on average but remains close in AUC; it is the best model on Vancomycin Balanced Accuracy (0.897) and the weakest on Amikacin (0.606 BalAcc / 0.729 AUC), where the few resistant samples make the threshold fragile.

  • Best-resolved drugs: Imipenem (0.89 BalAcc / 0.96 AUC), Cefepime (0.84 / 0.90) and Amoxicillin-Clavulanic acid (0.82 / 0.89); the hardest are Ceftazidime (0.70 / 0.78), Ciprofloxacin (0.72 / 0.80) and Amikacin (0.72 / 0.84).

  • Class weighting (07-02) is close to neutral on average: 0.785 vs 0.784 BalAcc for the unweighted MLP, while AUC improves on 9 of 10 drugs (mean 0.854 vs 0.848). The gains concentrate on the rarest classes — see the class-weight ablation in Results and Outputs.

  • These pooled numbers are the centralised upper bound for the federated study (08); the federated models aim to recover them without pooling raw data.
