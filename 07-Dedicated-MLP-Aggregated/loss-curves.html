Loss Curves for the 07 Hyperparameters

07 does not persist per-epoch training histories or MLP weights. To visualise how the configurations selected in 07 behave, two diagnostics retrain the same hyperparameters with MaldiMLPClassifier and log every epoch:

  • Pooled (02-01-Loss-Curves.ipynb): pooled A+B+C+D, 70/15/15, 50 epochs with a 10-epoch warmup.

  • A-only (03-01-Loss-Curves.ipynb): DRIAMS-A only, 80/20, for the cross-site diagnostic.

The curves are representative of the selected configurations, not bit-identical to the 07 models, whose custom PyTorch loop trains up to 100 epochs with cosine T_max=90 and starts from the best grid combination.

  • The AUROC panel peaks later than the loss minimum for Ciprofloxacin (0.766 at the best-loss epoch vs 0.808 at epoch 15) and Gentamicin (0.849 vs 0.863), while for Amoxicillin-Clavulanic acid the two coincide (0.897): checkpoint selection based on loss can stop slightly before the best-ranking model.
