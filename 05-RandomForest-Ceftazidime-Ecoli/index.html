05 — Random Forest: Ceftazidime Resistance in E. coli

Species-specific, drug-specific Random Forest classifier.

Objective

Introduce Random Forest as a third model family (after LogisticRegression in 01 and MLP in 02). Evaluated on a single well-populated drug–pathogen combination.

Scope

  • Drug: Ceftazidime

  • Pathogen: Escherichia coli (the most common species in DRIAMS)

  • Data: All 4 sites, 6000 bins at 3 Da resolution

Analysis Steps

  1. Load and filter: Ceftazidime data from all 4 sites, retain only E. coli

  2. Hyperparameter tuning: RandomizedSearchCV on pooled A+B+C+D with 20 iterations over:

    • n_estimators: [100, 200, 300, 500]

    • max_depth: [10, 20, 30, None]

    • min_samples_leaf: [2, 5, 10]

    • Class weight: [‘balanced’, ‘balanced_subsample’]

  3. Cross-site evaluation: Cross-site evaluation — train on DRIAMS-A, test on B/C/D separately (same pattern as 03)

  4. Multi-seed pooled evaluation: 5× random 75/25 splits on pooled A+B+C+D for robust error estimates

  5. Feature importance: Feature importance — which m/z bins drive resistance prediction?

Key Findings

  • Random Forest achieves competitive but slightly lower performance than MLP

  • Feature importance reveals specific spectral regions associated with ceftazidime resistance (β-lactamase-related peak shifts)

  • Aggregated (pooled) training substantially outperforms Cross-site evaluation, consistent with 03’s findings
