Federated Ceftriaxone × E. coli — Results

Flower (flwr)-based Federated learning across 4 DRIAMS sites (A/B/C/D): 50 rounds, 5 strategies (FedAvg MLP, FedProx μ=0.5 and 1.0, FedLR and FedRF).

These results were produced by the 2026-10-15 run of 06-03c-Species-Masked-Ceftriaxone-Federated.ipynb — see 06-03c: How It Works for the 4-phase pipeline. The Ceftazidime counterpart lives in 06a.

Note

Provenance. Run of 2026-10-15 (…-z-Runs/01-Run), 50 rounds, with the canonical squared FedProx penalty ((μ/2)·‖w − w_global‖²) and a worst-site-tuned RF decision threshold. Best configurations from best_params_used.txt: MLP lr 1.5e-4, dropout 0.8 (threshold 0.25); LR C 5e-5 (threshold 0.50); RF 200 trees / depth 20 / leaf 10 / balanced (threshold 0.23). Winning species mask: none. A companion run (06-03d) repeats the pipeline with site/instrument masking — see Mask & Deconfounding Diagnostics — Why Masking Fails.

Key Findings

Federated LR Leads

FedAvg LR is the strongest federated strategy on this drug–pathogen pair: it climbs from the chance-level initial evaluation to 0.743 BalAcc by round 35 and still holds 0.737 at round 50 — it never collapses, unlike the MLP strategies. It edges out the FedAvg MLP (0.724, peak round 27, then drifts to 0.668 by round 50) and lands within ~1 point of the unmasked centralized MLP (0.751) and ~2.3 points of the best masked centralized variant (union, 0.766). That ordering is the opposite of the dedicated per-drug study (07), where pooling favours the non-linear models.

Proximal Regularisation Hurts at μ ≥ 0.5

With the corrected squared FedProx penalty, both proximal strengths land below plain FedAvg: μ=0.5 reaches 0.688 (peak round 48) and μ=1.0 stalls at 0.510 — essentially chance. AUC-ROC stays high in both cases (0.808 / 0.804), so ranking quality survives; what collapses is the thresholded decision. High μ keeps the clients close to the global weights, compressing the probability scale against the shared validation-tuned threshold (0.25). The training-loss trace shows the same effect — by round 50 the pooled client cross-entropy is 0.043 for FedAvg, 0.176 for μ=0.5 and 0.464 for μ=1.0.

This is a protocol-sensitive finding: an earlier implementation (unsquared proximal distance, 30 rounds, μ=0.1) suggested a marginal FedProx benefit. With the canonical penalty and these μ values the trade-off is not favourable — proximal regularisation slows local fitting more than it buys in global stability.

Species Masking in Centralized Baselines

Each site trains a species-Random Forest and flags its top-500 species-predictive m/z bins; the four strategies differ in how those flags are combined. The flagged bins are zeroed, not used to filter species (see Species masking for the full definitions):

none

No masking — the full 6000-bin spectrum (the reference).

union

Bins flagged by at least one site.

majority

Bins flagged by at least two sites.

persite

Each site zeroes its own flagged bins, simulating local species distributions.

On the centralized MLP, union is the strongest (0.766), ahead of majority (0.761) and the unmasked baseline (0.751); persite is the weakest (0.727). The federated pipeline nevertheless selected none: its criterion is the worst-site FedAvg improvement over unmasked, and no mask improved the worst site.

06-03c: How It Works

The results on this page come from 06-03c-Species-Masked-Ceftriaxone-Federated.ipynb, a 4-phase notebook that works on all species (not just E. coli):

  1. Per-site species RF classifiers — identifies the top 500 m/z bins most predictive of species on each site

  2. Mask computation — three strategies (union/majority/persite) plus an unmasked baseline; each mask zeroes species-specific bins

  3. Mask comparison — FedAvg MLP trained on all four masked variants; winning mask = highest worst-site improvement over unmasked

  4. Full FL pipeline — winning mask applied, then FedAvg MLP, FedProx μ=0.5 and 1.0, FedLR, FedRF, and the cross-site baselines

Output: mask_delta.pdf (per-site improvement per mask vs. unmasked), the results table above, per-round CSV files (validation metrics and training loss) and the saved species RF classifiers for reuse in 08.

The winning mask for this run was ``none`` (no masking), meaning species-filtering did not improve worst-site performance on this drug–pathogen pair. The follow-up diagnostic (Mask & Deconfounding Diagnostics — Why Masking Fails) shows this is not a weak-mask artifact — species information is redundant across the spectrum, so zeroing the top-500 species bins removes almost no species signal regardless of the RF’s max_features. The companion site-masking run (06-03d) reaches the same conclusion for the instrument signal.

Training Loss Monitoring

The 06-03c notebook captures per-site training loss (cross-entropy) at every round alongside the validation metrics. This enables:

  • Overfitting diagnosis — comparing train vs. validation loss curves to detect when a model starts memorising local data

  • Site-specific training dynamics — identifying which hospitals learn fastest/slowest, revealing data quality or size differences

  • Proximal regularisation effect — quantifying how FedProx μ influences local optimisation vs. standard FedAvg (here: higher μ clearly slows the local fit — see the μ results above)

Training loss is extracted directly from each client’s fit() return value (which already computes per-epoch cross-entropy). For FedProx, this is captured via the custom CheckpointFedProx.aggregate_fit override, which records per-site and aggregated losses before model aggregation. For FedAvg, a fit_metrics_aggregation_fn is registered on the strategy to collect the mean training loss across clients.

Threshold Tuning Resolves the FedRF Stuck-at-0.5 Issue

FedRF (tree collection over 5 rounds, 1200 → 6000 trees) reaches 0.658 BalAcc / 0.815 AUC with the worst-site tuned threshold (0.23). An untuned 0.5 threshold had previously left its Balanced Accuracy pinned at 0.500 while AUC looked healthy — a pure calibration artifact on the imbalanced test set. Tuning the same way also lifts the cross-site RF baseline to 0.683 / 0.755, close to its federated counterpart.

Cross-Site as Lower Bound

Training on DRIAMS-A alone and testing on B/C/D yields 0.610 BalAcc (MLP) and 0.683 (RF). This is the cost of not sharing data at all: federated learning recovers 13 points on the MLP side (0.743 vs. 0.610) without centralising raw spectra. The RF baseline is the exception — with a tuned threshold its cross-site score is close to the federated tree collection.
