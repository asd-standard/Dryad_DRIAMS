Non-Federated Baselines

Ceftriaxone was studied through its federated runs; unlike Ceftazidime (06a analysis) there is no dedicated 06-00 / 06-01 / 06-02-style non-federated notebook. The non-federated evidence comes from the centralized and cross-site baselines computed inside the federated runs:

  • E. coli-only run (2026-07-17, results_e_coli_only/) — the 06-03a/06-03b lineage, restricted to E. coli spectra.

  • All-species run (2026-07-19, results/) — the 06-03c species-masked pipeline documented on Federated Ceftriaxone × E. coli — Results.

Centralized baseline training

The centralized MLP baselines quoted on Federated Ceftriaxone × E. coli — Results (unmasked and species-masked variants) train with plain cross-entropy on the pooled training split: up to 100 epochs with a 10-epoch warmup and cosine T_max=90, checkpoint selected every 5 epochs on the loss computed on the training loader (patience 3), i.e. without a separate validation split. The centralized RF has no loss (Gini impurity) and is selected by grid search; per-drug thresholds come from the external validation split. Because this checkpoint rule differs from the 02/03/04 MLP runs (internal 10% validation split), absolute loss values are not comparable across analyses — compare dynamics within the same protocol.

E. coli-only federated re-run (FedLR, 2026-10-17)

The E. coli-only FedLR arm was re-run with the corrected protocol (06-03b-Re-Run-lr-Ceftriaxone-E-coli-FIXED.ipynb): 8 SAGA iterations per round instead of 1, per-site worst-site C and decision threshold, same 30 rounds and splits. FedLR moves from 0.523 → 0.698 Balanced Accuracy (AUC 0.724 → 0.761) and becomes the best federated strategy of that level (FedAvg MLP 0.680, FedProx μ=0.5 0.677, FedRF 0.677), ahead of the centralized MLP (0.670) and just below the centralized RF (0.702). Per site: A 0.737, B 0.853, C 0.632, D 0.700 — C remains the weakest, though it improves from 0.519. The curve is still rising at round 30 (more rounds would likely help); 30 were kept for comparability with the original run. Results overwrite results_e_coli_only/.

The earlier flat FedLR (~0.5) was a protocol artefact: one warm-started SAGA iteration per round is far from convergence, and the decision threshold was untuned.

Findings

  • Ceftriaxone is the easier pair centrally. All-species, the centralized MLP gains +0.146 BalAcc over Ceftazidime (0.761 vs 0.615); in the E. coli-only comparison the RF is clearly stronger on Ceftriaxone (+0.042 BalAcc, +0.059 AUC), with a notably high Site B score (0.912).

  • Cross-site transfer is poor for both pairs (pooled BalAcc 0.50–0.61, AUC 0.50–0.68): the Domain shift cost is not specific to one drug, and single-site training is insufficient in both 06a and 06b.

  • The gap is one of packaging, not data. Ceftriaxone’s non-federated numbers exist as baselines inside its federated runs; Ceftazidime additionally has a dedicated non-federated program (06-00/06-01/06-02).

Scope

There is no 06-02-style aggregated iterative comparison (LR / MLP / RF with warm starts on a fixed test set) for Ceftriaxone; the tables above are the complete existing non-federated evidence. Producing the iterative counterpart would require a new run.
